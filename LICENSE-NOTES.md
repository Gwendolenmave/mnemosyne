## Why this licence and not an open-source one

Mnemosyne is memory infrastructure for personal AI systems. A deployment may
hold intimate personal facts, relationship history, private projects, and the
evidence from which those memories were formed. A permissive licence would
invite commercial hosted repackaging, while a hosted memory service has a very
different relationship to a person's data than the local-first system this
project is designed to protect.

This project is maintained by one person. A licence that invites commercial
adoption also creates support, review, provenance, and continuity obligations.
Declining those obligations up front is more honest than accepting them and
failing to meet them.

The trade is deliberate: this is **not** an OSI-approved open-source licence,
and calling it one would be wrong. Source-available and noncommercial is what
it is.
