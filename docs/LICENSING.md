# Licensing notes

This page explains the project's licensing boundary in plain language. The official terms are in [`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md); if anything here conflicts with the official license, the official license controls.

## What the licence allows

Mnemosyne is source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. In practical terms, noncommercial use is permitted, including personal use, study, experimentation, modification, and noncommercial sharing subject to the official terms.

Commercial use requires separate permission from the licensor.

PolyForm Noncommercial is a source-available licence rather than an OSI-approved open-source licence.
